## Developed by DigixArt Entertainment | Published under THQ Nordic
## UnrealEngine | C++ | Gameplay & UI Programmer

{{<youtubeLite id="miLQSW3n1jA" label="Tides of Tomorrow Pre-Order Trailer">}}

A narrative multiplayer adventure set on a post-apocalyptic ocean world where humanity struggles to survive on floating settlements.

## My Contributions:

* Gameplay: Built 4 core gameplay features from scratch (Stealth, Chase, Carry, Donation Box) and designed the initial architecture for the game’s USP, the Asynchronous Gameplay System, delivering a modular, scalable C++ framework that accelerated designer content iteration across the project. 
* UI: Owned end-to-end UI development as sole UI programmer, engineering 5+ dynamic menu screens and 20+ reusable HUD elements in close collaboration with Art & Design to deliver a high-fidelity, ship-ready interface. 
* Tools: Engineered 2 custom in-engine productivity tools (an integrated JIRA bug reporter and an NPC teleport utility) that eliminated context-switching, saving the team hours of manual bug-triage time per week.
